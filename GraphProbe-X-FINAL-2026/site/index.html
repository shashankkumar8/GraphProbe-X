Evidence

Contract · ledger · judge

Evidence Contract

Each question defines required fact slots up front. The contract makes the benchmark fair because every pipeline has to prove the same set of claims before the answer is sufficient.

Evidence Ledger

Claims are backed by verbatim quotes or raw field equality. Every status is explicit: SUPPORTED, PARTIAL, UNSUPPORTED, CONTRADICTED, or UNRESOLVED.

Evidence Judge

Coverage is deterministic: required slots are scored, contradictions block sufficiency, and unsupported claims are not silently papered over.

Failure Taxonomy

Brittle retrieval and escalation log
ENTITY

Missing entity grounding despite a near-match retrieval result. Usually caused by fuzzy search without explicit link verification.

RELATION

Evidence contains a matching entity but misses the relationship path needed to answer the causal or comparative question.

COVERAGE

Answer is partially supported but fails to cover all required slots, which triggers extra planning instead of premature completion.

GOVERNOR

Escalation beyond budget or below gain threshold is rejected before the LLM can override the hard stop conditions.

Ingest

Corpus / chunk / entity graph

Sources

Public corpus documents are ingested into the same indexing substrate used for all pipelines, preserving provenance from the raw source into the chunk and graph layer.

Chunks

Indexed by BM25 and semantic dense retrieval and then linked to entities, relations, and evidence claims in the TigerGraph layer.

Auditability

Every final result can be reconstructed from the saved run, ledger, and benchmark manifest. The hidden-gold guard prevents tuning on the evaluation set.
