# Round-2 Extension Blueprint

This document outlines structural extensions for post-hackathon Round-2 development:

1. **Conflicting & Superseding Facts Engine:** Extending the Evidence Ledger with temporal edge validity (`valid_from`, `valid_until`) to resolve historical supersessions.
2. **Source Authority & Provenance Weighting:** Weighting official Olympic results documents higher than general news summaries in contradiction disputes.
3. **Uncertainty Quantification:** Modeling Bayesian confidence scores over partially supported contract slots.
4. **Learned Governor Policy:** Replacing rule-based utility heuristics with a reinforcement-learned action policy trained on token-cost trade-offs.
